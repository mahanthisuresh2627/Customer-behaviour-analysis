# 📊 Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

This dashboard provides an interactive analysis of customer purchasing behaviour, helping businesses understand customer segments, product performance, and revenue trends.

## Dashboard KPIs

- Total Customers: 3.9K
- Average Purchase Amount: $59.76
- Average Review Rating: 3.75

## Dashboard Visuals

- Customer Subscription Distribution
- Revenue by Category
- Sales by Category
- Revenue by Age Group
- Sales by Age Group

## Interactive Filters

- Subscription Status
- Gender
- Category
- Shipping Type

## Business Insights

- Clothing category generates the highest revenue and sales.
- Most customers are non-subscribers (73%).
- Young adults contribute the highest revenue.
- Customer purchase behaviour varies across product categories and shipping methods.
